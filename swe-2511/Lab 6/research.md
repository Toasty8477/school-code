Charts.js is an open source charting library that uses the HTML5 canvas to draw charts. Charts.js can be installed using npm or added to a page using a CDN link found on their website.

Charts.js supports:
* custom styling like colors, font, etc.
* Primatives, arrays, and objects as data types.
* Animations for your charts.
* responsive chart sizing.
* Area, Bar, Bubble, Pie, Line, Polar Area, Radar, and Scatter chart types.
* Mixing of chart types.
* And More!

#### Advantages
* Charts.js is open source, development won't stop because a company stops supporting it.
* Google charts does not include any accessability features.
* Charts.js has more customizability than google charts.
#### Disadvantages
* Google charts has the ability to use VML for internet explorer, Charts.js does not.
* Charts.js charts rely on the developer to make them accessable.
* Charts.js supports less chart types than google charts.
